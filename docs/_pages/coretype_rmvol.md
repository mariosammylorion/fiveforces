# Motivation Development

The RMVOL individual is confident in their personal values, even from an early age. This can be caused by a myriad of reasons, including being around the less fortunate, seeing some other perceived injustices, or just having a heightened sense of empathy. But for some unexplained reason, they feel as if they have the power to influence the world with their values, but they feel as though they cannot do this alone. Eventually, they learn that they can rally others up behind their cause. That way, they can easily influence the masses and spread their agenda, forming the foundations of their psyche.

There are two side effects to this. The first of these effects involves the feelings these individuals obtain from having to sustain these passionate values. Because of the need for them to be spread, they become hyper-defensive of their own values. Not just that, but they sympathize with the dreams and desires of others, becoming the people's #1 activist, defending what they truly want in life. The morality of the outside ideas and concepts they defend does not matter, so long as the RMVOL's personal values are accounted for in their vision for a better world.

However, there is a nuance to this defense of the people, which brings us to this second side effect of their values. At the end of the day, the RMVOL individual is an activist for their own subjective opinions, only using the ideas of others to spread them. As such, it is an activism born of pure selfishness, which they are willing to pursue in spite of the consequences…so much so that they eschew any logical or abstract analysis. In fact, in their uncaring attitude towards others' objectivity, they actively seek to twist and break these expectations. No amount of explaining can deter them from getting what they want.

In wanting to defy logical expectations, they turn to something that can grab anyone's attention: by making their presence known in reality. No thought of theirs about the facts and experiences of themselves and others goes unnoticed in their dominance of the environment, which they command with an unmatched articulation. This also results in a heightened sense of enthusiasm for others' ideas, which they feel they can use to their advantage in the present moment. This, coupled with the passion of wanting to spread their values through others, makes these individuals true champions of others' ideas, even if it is for personal gain.

All of this meshes together to form one of the most dynamic core types in the entire Five Forces system. They ignite themselves and others with true passion, always willing to bend societal norms and expectations in order to appeal to the masses. They are ever-changing and ever-evolving, looking towards the future for any new outside ideas they can advocate for. Insecure about absolutely nothing and unapologetic in every single one of their endeavors, they have the power to turn any dull and boring dream into something that most people can get behind, changing their environment and the world for the better.

## Deca Influence

Like all other Quarta types, the RMVOL is a master at altering their environment that aligns with their sense of Imagination. They are unique in this aspect, however, in that they alter their own immediate physical environment, along with their Co-Reality type, RMOVL. They shake up their world with the visions and dreams of others, always ensuring that they make these visions a reality and find an ideology that not only aligns with their values, but challenges the establishment in a way that future RMVOL individuals can use as inspiration for their own activism, paving the way for further change in the world.

The RMVOL type features the characteristic ideological devotion found in the Quarta Deca. However, it is the only type in it to feature the de-elevation of Selfishness. Therefore, it is the only Quarta type whose devotion to outside ideas is a product born of their own inner desires to get what only they want. They are devoted, yes, but only because they want to use the dreams of others to justify their own values, as they see it as more valid than just going for what they want as is. In their eyes, as long as they are doing something good, they feel it is right for them to indulge in their own wants.

Still, it is quite common to see the RMVOL type represented in various activists throughout modern history. Although all types with Activism are common in these types of individuals, the RMVOL is the most outwardly dynamic and bold out of all of them. In fact, their activism leans much more into an outward defiance of the establishment. Though this is a common Quarta type behavior, this type reaches new heights with it, sometimes going as far as to commit violence just to get their point across. However, many prefer just to make themselves known to the right people in order to be seen and heard.
