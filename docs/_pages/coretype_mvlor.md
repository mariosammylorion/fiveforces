# Motivation Development

Early on, the MVLOR individual may live their lives without any bells or strings attached to it. They may have successful lives, whether through school or other means, or difficult ones. Yet one thing remains the same: eventually, these individuals get the sense that there is, perhaps, something that is missing in their life. They cannot put their finger on it at first, but this thing turns out to be something to believe in: a muse, a guardian, that can turn their lives upside-down for the better. They do not know this initially, but once they do find something to live for, the psyche truly begins to develop.

Once they find their muse, the MVLOR is a passionate defender of it, and two things occur when this takes place. The first is that their creativity, initially repressed, becomes inspired enough for its expression to be pushed outward. Except that since their muse is what drove this action in the first place, their Imagination's expression is chock full of musings and artistry for their one and only guardian. The second thing is, once these individuals find their place with their muse, they form their own objective opinions of it, free from the influence of those around them.

With their Imagination being awakened, there comes a time when these individuals spend so much time with it that it becomes a detriment to them being in the real world. In fact, it eventually gets to a point where the individual is completely in their own fantastical world with their guardian, detached from all sense of facts and experiences brought upon by other people. Not only that, but they grow to absolutely hate what other people have to offer with the Force of Reality, as any kind of factual information, past and present, may cause them to be broken away by the thing they love the absolute most.

This sense of being hateful towards Reality gives them a unique expression of their logic and objective opinions. With the objective opinions that the MVLOR individual has of their muse that were brought upon by their devotion, they now claim these opinions as entirely their own, not ever changing them to conform to anyone's standards, even their own. Hell, they don't even dare as to share these opinions with anyone. They are quite logical individuals, but said logic is completely, entirely their own.

With the rest of their Atoms fully developed, their Body completely sets everything in stone and paints a unique picture. The MVLOR individual is a passionate artist driven by something that they believe in: a muse, a guardian. They want to share all of their art and creations with the world around them, always heeding to this devotion. Yet at the same time, they do not wish to share the muse themselves. They have their own one-of-a-kind thoughts on what this muse actually is logically, and heaven forbid anyone, including reality itself, encroach upon it. It's a devotion that simply reads: "You can look, but you can't touch."

## Deca Influence

Though it may be hard to believe at first, the MVLOR type is focused first and foremost on task completion. This is an innate quality of all Octava types, but with this type, it definitely does not appear that way at first. Does this, therefore, make their love ingenuine? Not quite. While their love is real, their love is a different kind of expression unlike most. They express their gratitude through the completion of their muse's will; every single novel idea they create and every opinion they have, they see as tasks to be completed in order to fully appease their object of devotion.

Because of the Octava Deca's influence, MVLOR individuals are not at all people-pleasers, especially since they have the aforementioned hatred of others' expressions of Reality. In their eyes, the only one truly deserving to be pleased is their object of affection. They will do anything for their muse, including making up excuses as to why they behave in certain ways, and put on any form of emotions or behaviors that reflects their love for this muse. It is very common for these individuals to, for example, change up the way they dress, write, or express themselves, even if it is just the little things.

Perhaps a great strength of the MVLOR being this devoted is their raw, emotional honesty and their sincere expression when it comes to their emotions. Many times, these individuals see their muse as life guides who want them to be happy, healthy, and want them to succeed in life. One of the ways that this is accomplished is through the channeling of their emotions to genuine expression. They are happy when they want to be happy, they cry when they want to cry, and when they get angry, it is a raw and genuine anger that is felt through the entire room. This makes it rare for them to lie or to say things they don't truly mean.
