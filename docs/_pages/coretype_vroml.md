# Motivation Development

Because the VROML's motivation operates under a loop, any Atom can develop first in the individual. However, the Withdrawn Atom is usually developed first, so an example of that shall be given.

Early on, the VROML's relationship with the new and the novel is not the best, to put it lightly. There may have been several occasions where in fact, it has failed them miserably. These individuals do not put any blame on any kind of special force or individual; in fact, the only person that they blame for being unprepared for the future is themselves. However, they do not wish to make this fear and self-loathing known. And so they put up a happy demeanor that, while genuine, they constantly feel the need to perfect it in order to act like everything is fine and dandy when in reality, it's not.

What's more, the VROML goes to great lengths just to keep up this behavior at all times. In fact, often times they feel as if a nice and friendly demeanor is not enough. As such, they turn to ferocity and insistent behavior in order to keep their spirits alive and distracted from the unknown that awaits them. This eventually gets to the point where these individuals eschew logical analysis and the abstract itself, as it is a distraction from this distraction. But although they don't care to be intellectual themselves...they absolutely love it when others are.

In their admiration of intellect from afar, they always want to hear what others have to say from a detached logical and factual perspective. In fact, their respect for those who have intellectual authority goes as far as for these individuals to think that *everyone* has this authority. As such, it creates a situation where they will listen to anyone on any kind of subject that requires deep, analytical thought. To them, nobody is stupid, nobody is a fool, and everyone deserves to be listened to.

But eventually, this behavior gets to a point where it is too much for the VROML individual to bear. Because of their always-open ears, they subject themselves to outside facts (whether actually true or not) that can have negative consequences for them. In particular, they may come across information that actually confirms their fears of the future. This only makes them more vigilant of changes in their life, which only influences their bubbly yet ferocious demeanor further. Due to these obtained facts, they can also take on a more timid nature in combination with this ferocity they have developed much earlier.

The development of their Optimization gives rise to a truly contradictory type. Ferocious, yet timid; and always listening, yet afraid of the wrong information being said. This creates a psyche in which extra time and consideration is necessary to make any kind of decision in life, whether it is major life decisions such as their career, or even such minor things as what to wear for the day and what to eat for dinner. Do not let their happy-go-lucky and naive demeanor fool you at all; deep down, the VROML is afraid, and terrified of what is to come. And if they are not careful, it can lead to complete analysis paralysis and the shutdown of the entire psyche.

## Deca Influence

Due to the influence of the Duodecima Deca, the VROML has a sense of grounded conviction that everything they are doing, whether it is obtaining information or using their values, is for the betterment of themselves and others. They often deny being in pain thanks to their Camouflage, saying things like "Oh, I'm fine" and "It's no big deal" when...actually, it *is* a big deal. This can very obviously result in a dangerous mindset in which their suffering is ignored just to make other people happy that they themselves are seemingly happy...even though hiding their pain can make others unhappy as well.

The VROML individual also, like all other Duodecima types, feels the need to be prepared for any kind of situation that may arise. The fact that they have 4M only makes their hypervigilance of what could go wrong more pronounced; in fact, one possible behavior of representatives of this type is doomsday prepping (yes, multiple core types do this besides the VORML), only basing their information about a possible apocalypse from a random AI-generated article they saw on Facebook. I know this is a hyper-specific example, but it is a valid one worth mentioning.

It should also be worth mentioning that the VROML type fundamentally does all of these things so that their ideology can fully be realized in the world. To them, there is no remedy to their pain unless they are seen and heard. It is a stereotype that Duodecima types can be seen as "attention whores," but VROML individuals crave attention to the point of hiding their pain just to fit into society, including listening to all other individuals' logical arguments, no matter how invalid they are. As we will see later, there are several other behaviors that they possess just to grab others' attention, many of them unhealthy.
