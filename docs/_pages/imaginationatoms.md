# 2M - Selfless Imagination / "Consumer"

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

Perhaps one of the most common expressions of 2M in both real-life persons and fictional characters is their tendency to hop on the bandwagon of popular trends. If there is a new trend, idea, or meme that is gaining traction, the Consumer will most likely embrace this idea without a second thought. And so, there is a crucial difference between those that have 1M or 2M: **Revolutionaries are trend *setters*, while Consumers are trend *followers*.**
