Recall that for each of the five Forces that an individual possesses, there are different levels and types of energy that is created and transferred to and from these Forces. And just like with the Five Forces themselves, the combination of two different aspects of this energy creates five different combinations. These combinations take up a different "slot" in the human mind, and therefore are called **positions.**

These five positions are as follows:
1. The combination of a **Self Source** and an **Outward Direction** produces an energy characterized by a large sense of confidence in the Force and a desire to make said confidence known to the world. This forms the basis of the **Mutual position,** which is represented as the **1st** position or with **1.**
2. The combination of an **Others Source** and an **Outward Direction** produces an energy characterized by a downplaying or modesty with skills that require the Force, but still a desire to master them. This forms the basis of the **Selfless position,** which is represented as the **2nd** position or with **2.**
3. The combination of a **Self Source** and an **Inward Direction** produces an energy characterized by a large sense of confidence in the Force, but a desire to keep said confidence hidden from the world. This forms the basis of the **Egocentric position,** which is represented as the **3rd** position or with **3.**
4. The combination of an **Others Source** and an **Inward Direction** produces an energy characterized by a downplaying or self-deprecation with skills that require the Force and a private insecurity with them. This forms the basis of the **Withdrawn position,** which is represented as the **4th** position or with **4.**
5. Finally, the combination of **No Source** and **No Direction** produces no energy at all, forming the **Apathetic position,** which is represented as the **5th** position or with **5.**

# The Five Positions in Detail

## The Mutual Position (1)

The **Mutual position** is formed from the combination of a self energy source and an outward energy direction. To start us off, it should be obvious that when we start our lives, we have a certain speciality when it comes to what actions we take. It can come from any place of origin, but one thing for certain is that we ourselves started it. It is just that special spark that ignites all of us, one that we love doing and consider ourselves experts at. But what good is a special skill if we do not utilize it? The human psyche, understanding this, attempts to do so in perhaps the most efficient way possible: by sharing our knowledge and expertise in hopes that others will also see them as "the expert."

From a very early age when we discover what we are excellent at, we have the idea that we, quote, "have the power." What this exact power is, however, is up to the individual. But whatever it is decided as, this power comes in the form of changing our environment, our world, and even other people to suit our desires. Whether for good or for bad, all individuals deep down want their mastery of their craft known all throughout their circle. If others see them as the expert, then they know they have truly succeeded in life. The individual's happiness, therefore, is derived from this ultimate goal. And it defines the key characteristic of the Mutual position: **expertise.**

## The Selfless Position (2)

The **Selfless position** is formed from the combination of an others energy source and an outward energy direction. It is initially characterized by a deep interest in how the Force that occupies it manifests in the world. They are initially indifferent to their own abilities in said Force but derive great joy in seeing others use it, for better or worse. The person initially feels that they will never have what it takes to become experts in this Force, but now that their psyche is developed, they have moved on from this indifference and now seek to become experts, wanting to share their knowledge, experiences, values, etc. to anyone who is willing to listen. Unfortunately, they do not currently possess the current skills for it, nor have they practiced trying despite having full exposure to it from the outside. And so, the psyche of this position defaults to expending their energy the only way they know how: outward.

And with this outward energy comes a time when an authority figure (or multiple authority figures) in this Force is found. This figure can comprise anything outside the person's own psyche: it can be another person or people, it could be something indirect like a book or another piece of media, it could be some other object in the world, or it could even be something as simple as a concept or an idea. And so before the eye can even blink completely, the psyche latches onto this authority with an iron grip. And with this comes trust, energy, validation, security, admiration, and most importantly, *outside knowledge that they can share*...all in this one authority figure. Which gives way to the key characteristic of the Selfless position: **deference.**

Simply put, the Selfless position provides people with happiness in a Force they know they will never master on their own. They will do anything for this authority in the Force, without a second thought. What the authority figures do, the psyche does as well. This creates a sort-of dependence on this figure in matters relating to the Force. The Selfless position constantly asks questions and seeks permission from the authority figures.

## The Egocentric Position (3)

The **Egocentric position** is formed from the combination of a self energy source and an inward energy direction. With the Mutual position, I have explained earlier that the individual often possesses an area of expertise that they would love to share with others. However, this absolutely does not mean that the individual only has *one* Force they are confident in. But because only one Force can occupy one position at a time, something must happen to the other Force that separates the two.

And this is exactly what happens: throughout their life, the individual often sees others utilizing what two Forces they are confident in. But for whatever reason...adverse childhood experiences, deeper insecurities, or just plain hatred; one of these Forces is warped in a way that makes the individual not care for or even shun others' expressions of it. With the Mutual position, this is not so much of a hatred; at the very least, it's an annoyance that they feel they can "cure" and guide others in the "truly right direction." But this Force is much more stingy, much more arrogant, and much less willing to share. It forms the key characteristic of the Egocentric position: **self-centeredness.**

## The Withdrawn Position (4)

The **Withdrawn position** is formed from the combination of an others energy source and an inward energy direction. When it first arises in the individual, the **Withdrawn position** is just like the Selfless position: an indifference to one's own abilities in the Force, but a great interest in seeing how it works in everyday situations. And so as they develop, they also want to become experts in the Force. Unfortunately, only one of these Forces can occupy the Selfless position. So what happens to the other?

It can very greatly vary depending on the individual. Some have had adverse childhood experiences; for example, they could have been manipulated or gaslit; or could have been bullied constantly; or have otherwise lived a dangerous and messy life. But sometimes, it doesn't take a rough life; one could live a completely normal one without any problems. But no matter what, the individual sees people's and the world's engagement with a particular Force. And somehow, someway, with the Withdrawn Force, *they just don't like what they see.* They internalize what they experienced from outside, and act as if not only that they will never have what it takes to engage with this Force completely, but they are scared that their thoughts on the Force will never live up to the world or society's expectations for it. This fear leads to a profound avoidance of any situation that uses the Force. Thus forms the key characteristic of this position: **disappointment.**

But they are so paranoid about what others might think that they feel like they cannot trust anyone or anything. It's like there's a little voice inside their head that says, "I want to, but I just can't." They hate to admit it, but these individuals secretly want to be experienced in the respective realm; as such, their behavior towards it is a form of projection.

## The Apathetic Position (5)

The **Apathetic position** is formed from the combination of no energy source and no energy direction. And with absolutely zero energy, it barely seems as if the Force even exists in the individual at all. But keep in mind: there are five Forces, and five positions, so this last position is just a process-of-elimination, last-man-standing situation. In summary, this position is the result of the individual's psyche delegating so many of their mental resources to the other four Forces that there is absolutely nothing to give to the fifth. As such, it is a realm of carelessness and, well, apathy. There really is no needed further explanation for how this gives way to the key characteristic of this position: **indifference.**

# Functions and Accents

Some positions feature common characteristics between each other. While these commonalities do always manifest somewhat, they are mostly made known through the interactions complementary Forces have with each other in the psyche. Human Units always take up two positions, and these positions' defining traits (which are usually subdued) are amplified in these Forces, a concept that is known as an **accent.** The characteristic that is amplified is part of what is called a **function.**

Expressions of the Five Forces in each position are NOT static. In any given core type, each position *belongs* to four different function groups, but a Force can only be expressed in one at a time. **The expression of said Force (that is, the function that is active) depends on the position of the Force that complements it.**

## The Ten Function Groups

### Public (1 + 2)

The Mutual and Selfless positions together form the **Public function group.** In both of these positions, the energy direction of these Forces is outward. As a result, Forces in this function group **manifest through public expression.** The individual chooses to share their expressions of these Forces, and in turn the people around them know that they possess these Forces in the position they are in.

### Authentic (1 + 3)

The Mutual and Egocentric positions together form the **Authentic function group.** In both of these positions, the energy source of these Forces is the self. As a result, Forces in this function group are those in which **the individual chooses to use, free from outside input.** Any and all decisions regarding these Forces are made via the individual's own accord and confidence.

### Processed (1 + 4)

The Mutual and Withdrawn positions together form the **Processed function group.** In both of these positions, the psychic energies start in one source and transfer to a different plane in which they started. As a result, Forces in this function group are those in which **the individual requires mental time, energy, and negotiation to make decisions on.**

### Uninhibited (1 + 5)

The Mutual and Apathetic positions together form the **Uninhibited function group.** In both of these positions, there is neither a source from others nor an inward destination, which makes it the exact opposite of the Withdrawn position. As a result, Forces in this function group are those in which **the individual can use with zero fear of outside judgment or ridicule.** Individuals with these Forces feel as if they can just not conform to societal or worldly expectations, as they have no anxiety regarding them.

### Natural (2 + 3)

The Selfless and Egocentric positions together form the **Natural function group.** In both of these positions, the psychic energies start in one source and transfer to the same plane in which they started. As a result, Forces in this function group are those in which **the individual requires little to no mental energy or negotiation to make decisions on.** Using these Forces comes naturally to the individual, hence the name.

### Recipient (2 + 4)

The Selfless and Withdrawn positions together form the **Recipient function group.** In both of these positions, the energy source of these Forces is through others. As a result, Forces in this function group **are put on the individual by the world.** Any and all decisions regarding these Forces are *not* of the individual's own accord. In a way, individuals with these Forces feel as if the world forces them to use them.

### Permissive (2 + 5)

The Selfless and Apathetic positions together form the **Permissive function group.** In both of these positions, there is neither a source from the self nor an inward destination, which makes it the exact opposite of the Egocentric position. As a result, Forces in this function group are those in which **there is no internal process or resistance for them.** There are no boundaries regarding these Forces, meaning that the individual can adopt or be swayed by whatever is put in front of them.

### Private (3 + 4)

The Egocentric and Withdrawn positions together form the **Private function group.** In both of these positions, the energy direction of these Forces is inward. As a result, Forces in this function group **are expressed privately, without others knowing.** The individual chooses to hide these expressions, mainly out of fear of ridicule, or others simply not understanding.

### Impervious (3 + 5)

The Egocentric and Apathetic positions together form the **Impervious function group.** In both of these positions, there is neither a source from others nor an outward destination, which makes it the exact opposite of the Selfless position. As a result, Forces in this function group **are completely immune to outside influence.**

### Deficient (4 + 5)

The Withdrawn and Apathetic positions together form the **Deficient function group.**  In both of these positions, there is neither a source from the self nor an outward destination, which makes it the exact opposite of the Mutual position. As a result, Forces in this function group are those in which **the individual struggles with or outright refuses to implement in the real world.**

## Accents of Each Position

### Mutual Position Accents

#### Public Mutual (1X-2) / "Receptive"

When a Mutual Force is paired with a Selfless one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of deference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual lacks a fear or anxiety of being proven wrong when it comes to their knowledge or expression of the Force.** In fact, they *love* being proven wrong about it, as this can give them opportunities for growth. The motto of this accent may be, "My knowledge *isn't* perfect."

#### Authentic Mutual (1X-3) / "Nonconformist"

When a Mutual Force is paired with an Egocentric one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of self-centeredness. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses an unwillingness to change their knowledge or expression of the Force to fit societal norms and expectations**, making their sharing of it possess an individualist streak. The motto of this accent may be, "My knowledge *is* perfect."

#### Processed Mutual (1X-4) / "Perfectionist"

When a Mutual Force is paired with a Withdrawn one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of disappointment. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses an inner anxiety and a sense of perfection when it comes to the Force**, making it so that they have to work harder mentally in order to get their knowledge or expression of it just right. The motto of this accent may be, "My knowledge *has* to be perfect."

#### Uninhibited Mutual (1X-5) / "Unfiltered"

When a Mutual Force is paired with an Apathetic one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of indifference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual completely lacks a filter between their thoughts and actions when it comes to the Force.** They possess any kind of knowledge or any kind of expression they want, and they share whatever is on their mind when it comes to that. The motto of this accent may be, "My knowledge *doesn't have* to be perfect."

### Selfless Position Accents

#### Public Selfless (2X-1) / "Passionate"

When a Selfless Force is paired with a Mutual one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of expertise. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual's expression of deference becomes outward and bold.** They are always ready to ask questions regarding the Force and apply others' knowledge or expressions. The motto of this accent may be, "I *love sharing* others' knowledge."

#### Natural Selfless (2X-3) / "Defensive"

When a Selfless Force is paired with an Egocentric one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of self-centeredness. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses an increased confidence in their own and others' abilities in the Force**. They actually possess it to the point where they become sensitive to criticism of *others'* abilities as well as their own, making it look solely like an Egocentric Force. The motto of this accent may be, "I *love defending* others' knowledge."

#### Recipient Selfless (2X-4) / "Docile"

When a Selfless Force is paired with a Withdrawn one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of disappointment. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses a calm, quiet, and almost sheepish deference to others' authority with the Force**, mainly out of anxiety or fear. The motto of this accent may be, "I *have to follow* others' knowledge."

#### Permissive Selfless (2X-5) / "Indulgent"

When a Selfless Force is paired with an Apathetic one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of indifference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual completely gathers outside information about the Force with zero filter or resistance.** They will *always* listen to what others have to say. This can result in them holding several contradictory facts, opinions, ideas, values, et cetera at once, making it seem like they don't care at first glance. The motto of this accent may be, "I *have to listen* to others knowledge."

### Egocentric Position Accents

#### Authentic Egocentric (3X-1) / "Sociable"

When an Egocentric Force is paired with a Mutual one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of expertise. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual has an increased willingness to meet more like-minded individuals when it comes to the Force.** Granted, they still keep the Force under wraps through themselves or with the select few in their "circle", but they want this circle to grow larger and larger. The motto of this accent may be, "I *want to expand* my circle."

#### Natural Egocentric (3X-2) / "Commanding"

When an Egocentric Force is paired with a Selfless one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of deference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual uses other people or influences to forward or spread their knowledge and expression of the Force.** The motto of this accent may be, "I *have to expand* my circle."

#### Private Egocentric (3X-4) / "Fearful"

When an Egocentric Force is paired with a Withdrawn one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of disappointment. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses an increased fear of judgment or ridicule when it comes to the Force**, so much as to not want to interact with anyone outside their circle. The motto of this accent may be, "I *have to hide* my circle."

#### Impervious Egocentric (3X-5) / "Stubborn"

When an Egocentric Force is paired with an Apathetic one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of indifference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses an absolute stubbornness and complete unwillingness to change or share their knowledge or expression of the Force for anyone or anything.** Most notably, they don't even want to *talk* about their knowledge. The motto of this accent may be, "I *want to hide* my circle."

### Withdrawn Position Accents

#### Processed Withdrawn (4X-1) / "Self-Deprecating"

When a Withdrawn Force is paired with a Mutual one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of expertise. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual blames themselves when it comes to their shortcomings with the Force.** This often manifests as sarcasm and/or giving the impression that they have it together. The motto of this accent may be, "I *deflect* my pain."

#### Recipient Withdrawn (4X-2) / "Projective"

When a Withdrawn Force is paired with a Selfless one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of deference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual blames others when it comes to their shortcomings with the Force.** The motto of this accent may be, "I *project* my pain."

#### Private Withdrawn (4X-3) / "Tenacious"

When a Withdrawn Force is paired with an Egocentric one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of self-centeredness. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual refuses to seek help or assistance when it comes to their struggles with the Force.** Although it may appear as if they enjoy the pain, it is more so a tolerance or outright denial of their own suffering. The motto of this accent may be, "I *tolerate* my pain," or alternatively, perhaps "I *deny* my pain."

#### Deficient Withdrawn (4X-5) / "Concealed"

When a Withdrawn Force is paired with an Apathetic one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of indifference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual tends to mask their struggles with the Force.** Whether they're actually good at it or not is, admittedly, up for debate. The motto of this accent may be, "I *hide* my pain."

### Apathetic Position Accents

#### Uninhibited Apathetic (5X-1) / "Opportunistic"

When an Apathetic Force is paired with a Mutual one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of expertise. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual bends the rules of society when it comes to the Force.** Like with the Mutual Force, individuals with this accent want to change the world with this Force...so ironically, they change the world by not caring. The motto of this accent may be, "I *don't care about* others' knowledge."

#### Permissive Apathetic (5X-2) / "Respectful"

When an Apathetic Force is paired with a Selfless one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of deference. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses great respect and admiration for individuals who use the Force, while not using it themselves.** This often manifests as the individual wanting to go along with others' expressions or knowledge of the Force. The motto of this accent may be, "I *love* others' knowledge."

#### Impervious Apathetic (5X-3) / "Antagonistic"

When an Apathetic Force is paired with an Egocentric one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of self-centeredness. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual possesses undue hatred and disengagement towards others expressions or knowledge of the Force.** This is not out of fear or trauma, but...just because. They tend to be sensitive when it comes to others' criticisms about not engaging with the Force. The motto of this accent may be, "I *hate* others' knowledge."

#### Deficient Apathetic (5X-4) / "Grudging"

When an Apathetic Force is paired with a Withdrawn one, it takes on the latter position's characteristic of disappointment. The result is a manifestation in which **the individual internalizes and is envious of others' skills in the Force**. They accept that they cannot ever be good at it, which results in them not understanding others' viewpoints on the Force. The motto of this accent may be, "I *don't understand* others' knowledge."
