# 1O - Mutual Optimization / "Compensator"

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**

  - They "compensate" for their poor self-image by outwardly validating it through others
- Self-Image: Insecure
- Others-Perception: Either cares or doesn't care about how others perceive them

## Recipient-Impervious 1O / "Strategy" (Neotype)

## Natural-Deficient 1O / "Confidence" (Countertype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Individuals with Confidence often appear, well...confident on the outside
- They compensate for their poor self-image by emphasizing their Natural Unit to put up a veneer of confidence, making them seem more secure in their self-image than they actually are
- This 1O subtype is the least like traditional 1O, hence this being the countertype
  - This type can appear to be another Body Type, including 2O or 4O...but make no mistake: beneath their confident demeanor is an insecure self-image

# 2O - Selfless Optimization / "Impenitent"

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

Because the Selfless position is occupied by a dummy variable in these individuals, they lack a tangible, expressive force that exhibits the core characteristic of deference. As a result, it can be stated that they do not defer to anyone for anything. This causes some unique characteristics in all 2O types, but most notably, this makes these individuals largely unconcerned with how they are viewed in the eyes of others. They tend to do things completely on their own whim, uncaring about how they are perceived, making for some pretty selfish individuals if unhealthy enough.

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Because Impenitents don't care about how others perceive them, this gives them a special behavior that they implement that may degrade their self-image, yet this gives them no worry
- "Manipulation" is a key word of this Atom; it is a manipulation of other people (except for one subtype)
- Self-Image: Either secure or insecure about their self-image
- Others-Perception: Doesn't care how others perceive them

## Authentic-Deficient 2O / "Obstruction" (Coretype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

  - "You do this for me" kind of stuff
- Called Obstruction because the individual feels as though they don't *have* to be skilled in their Deficient Unit since they don't care what others think, thus obstructing their ability to get anything meaningful done with said Unit

## Uninhibited-Private 2O / "Bravado" (Neotype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**

# 3O - Egocentric Optimization / "Primper"

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

For Impenitents, the lack of a tangible Selfless Force gave way to an indifference with how they are perceived by others. Because Primpers have no expressive Egocentric Force, they are not self-confident in anything to the point of not sharing it. And so, the opposite occurs: Primpers spend a great deal of energy just to perfect their self-image and how others perceive them. They want to be seen as good in the eyes of others, and so they work hard to build a sense of humility and goodness that may not actually be in them.

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Primpers spend a lot of time "primping" their own self-image, hence the name
- Self-Image: Either secure or insecure about their self-image
- Others-Perception: Cares about how others perceive them
  - Wants others to perceive them in an image they favor

## Processed-Permissive 3O / "Diligence" (Countertype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Individuals with this Body Type "primp" their own self-image by appearing hardworking and diligent, thus building up a good work ethic

  - ALL input is subject to time and mental energy to process
- "I'll have to think about it," except unironically and all the damn time

  - They might freeze up or panic due to them having to process too many things at once

- This Body Type is the countertype because it doesn't appear that they project themselves to be something that they aren't; it seems like they *want* to be more diligent and hardworking
  - And yet, they don't want to do it for the sake of just doing it; they're concerned about their image
  - Actually works towards what they want their image to be

## Public-Impervious 4O / "Superficiality" (Countertype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

The following is a metaphor for what best describes this subtype. Imagine a museum display featuring some object inside an unopenable, clear glass container. Because it is clear, you know for certain there is an object inside, that it exists, and that you can see it. There may also be some accompanying text near this display that describes the object and its importance.

And yet despite you knowing that it exists and that you can see it, you cannot touch it. You cannot know what it feels like. You cannot smell or taste it. And you absolutely cannot claim it as yours, or take it home for yourself. It eternally belongs to the museum, where they keep it safe right in that glass container.

This, my friends, is what Superficiality entails. It's almost like this subtype signifies a "dark side" to the overall personality, especially if unhealthy. You can clearly tell that this characteristic is there, and you can see it for yourself, but you will never know, experience, or possess it. If Superficiality had a motto, it would be: "You can look, but you can't touch."

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- This Body Type is the countertype because it makes it appear as if they are insecure in their self-image by putting their Impervious Unit behind a glass box
  - In actuality, they *are* confident, they just don't want others to touch it

# 5O - Apathetic Optimization / "Double Agent"

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

It is of note that **the mechanism and nature of 5O is inherently contradictory.**

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Self-Image: Either secure or insecure about their self-image
- Others-Perception: Either cares or doesn't care about how they're perceived by others
- These things fluctuate wildly; one day they might give one answer, but another day they might say a different answer
  - Because they fluctuate in their self-image and perception of others, the individual feels as if they have no choice but to delegate these feelings to two areas of life, separating them so that they don't feel overwhelmed

## Authentic-Recipient 5O / "Duplicity" (Coretype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Duplicity is a *separation of life*, making two completely different and distinct halves where one is Authentic and the other is Recipient
- This is what most people think of when they think of 5O, hence this being the coretype; *literal* definition of a double life

## Public-Private 5O / "Secrecy" (Neotype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

Recall that a museum display metaphor was used to describe the 4O body type "Superficiality." We can expand upon this further by introducing a display right next to that one, except this time, the glass container is replaced by a jet black box. When you look at this display, you only see the box. You cannot see what is inside of it, no matter how hard you try. You don't even know if there even *is* anything in the box, for that matter. So not only can you not touch it, smell it, or take it home or call it yours, you can't even see it or even know for sure it even exists in the first place. So in essence, while Superficiality and Secrecy both entail something you cannot have for yourself, only Secrecy gets away with not even being known.

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Secrecy is a *separation of the self*, making two different personas, one public and one private

## Processed-Natural 5O / "Specialization" (Countertype)

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor...&gt;

**Unorganized thoughts**
- Specialization is a *separation of tasks*, delegating tasks that require no effort and those that require more effort to separate areas
- In one area of life, the individual naturally comes to decisions and is always in flow; and in the other, the individual needs time and often struggles
- This is the countertype because it doesn't appear to lead to a "double life" of sorts; these individuals can be easily confused with other types
