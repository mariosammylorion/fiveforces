# Executive Summary

The LVORM gives way to perhaps one of the most loyal types in the entire Five Forces system. Though visibly and truly anxious, individuals of this type show their devotion through keeping their word and taking in all the pain that comes with life. Though they are slow to make decisions, their trust is unmatched, and they are willing to go out of their way to ensure that you have the support that you need. It is hard to earn their trust, but once you do, they become a loyal friend, a votive guardian, and someone that you can always count on. No matter what.

# Motivation Development

It is always complicated whenever an individual's first Atom to develop is 4R. There could be a whole wide array of things that could've happened in their lives that brought about this fear of the world, so no two core types are the same. But with the LVORM individual, this usually comes with the sense that there is nobody out there in the world that is caring for or providing for them. They develop a perceived sense of misfortune in their life that may or may not be there, which has a domino effect that ultimately leads to where they end up going: serving another with all of their love.

With their developed fear of Reality, the LVORM individual feels as though they constantly have to watch what they say, and to make sure their thoughts are absolutely perfect. They develop the gift of logical and objective analysis to compensate for their factual and experiential struggles, but it comes at the cost of developing an overall anxious demeanor and a fear to speak up when the time is right. In addition, their perceived bad luck leaves their mark on them, as they accept it to the point that they don't even bother to find their way out of it, leaving their sense of Imagination completely ignored.

However, they don't just *entirely* leave it to rot. In fact, though they lack creative skills, they don't mind others having them or using them. In fact, they greatly admire people who are imaginative, to the point where they obtain a great sense of happiness whenever these people are around them. Creative people are their only source of happiness in a world that constantly disappoints and even hurts them, to the point where they feel as if they need to give something back to them. This, coupled with their perfectionist sense of logic, gives them the idea...to serve.

Thus, they devote themselves to an outside source. It could be a religion, an ideology, another person, or anything else that they can find meaning in. Having this devotion reaps two benefits. The first is that the outside value system finally gives them the logical edge to perfect their analytical skills. The second is that those who develop ideologies are inherently creative people, and we all know how much the LVORM loves creative people. Both of these reasons create a devoted soul who is willing to do anything for their object of affection, for better or worse.

At last, their psyche fully develops. The LVORM individual is often slow on the draw. They often freeze up in chaotic situations and need extra processing time to make any kind of decision. But if anything, they are loyal and faithful to their cause. They are completely humble, always seeking answers from only their object of affection. In the end, extra time necessary to make decisions is only a small price these individuals pay, as they now have an ideology or person that not only gives them a sense of logical confidence, but provides protection from a harsh, confusing world.

## Deca Influence

All Tertia types are loyal, and the LVORM is no different. In fact, it may be one of, if not the most loyal type in the entire Deca. These individuals are completely bound by their object of affection, to the point where they are even willing to engage in unhealthy and problematic actions just to please them. This is a pitfall that all Tertia types can fall into, but it is especially devastating for the LVORM because the object or person they are attached to is their only answer to a world that confuses them. Being rejected or letting the object down, therefore, can be absolutely devastating to them.

The LVORM individual, like other Tertia types, is an honest one who is committed to the unadulterated truth and nothing but. They are often completely honest in their speech and mannerisms, even bluntly so sometimes. To them, the only way that they can be truly devoted to their object of affection is to give to them their honest, genuine self. As a result, not only are they not prone to lying to anyone, but it can actually be very hard for them to do so. Lying directly goes against their own objective and subjective principles; they can even be uncomfortable with *other* people lying as well!

It should once again be stated that the LVORM is absolutely willing to suffer just for their object of affection. (No duh, this is a Tertia type we're talking about.) But these individuals are some of the most extreme in this regard. Not even death can stop them from letting their chosen ideology or people that they truly love and care about them. They are even willing to completely go against the entire world, even though they are terrified of it, creating one of the best examples of a type that can easily "face their fears" when it comes to the Five Forces system.
