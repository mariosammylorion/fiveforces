# 3R - Egocentric Reality / "Relativist"

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor…&gt;

There is very much a distinction to be made between 3R and 1R. Whereas Tacticians are masters of reality, **Relativists are masters of *their* reality.** Tacticians see the world for what it actually is, **while Relativists see the world for what *they* think it is.**

&lt;Lorem ipsum dolor…&gt;

A ludicrous yet perfectly valid example of 3R is this: suppose a boy lived in a secluded mansion with his family, with no contact with the outside world. Now, suppose that this family had a bunch of cats, but they all exclusively stood and walked upright on their hind legs. And so throughout the boy's life, he consistently thought that cats exclusively walk upright, a viewpoint fueled by his own personal experiences. He is now grown up and finally out in the world, and he notices something strange to him: all of the cats he sees now are walking on all fours. *No, this can't be*, he thinks to himself. He asks about this to a stranger or an acquaintance, who says that cats exclusively walk on all fours. Instead of adjusting to account for the misinformation he received in his youth, he doubles down and says, "Well, that's not what I've experienced! Prior to coming here, all the cats I've seen walk on two legs!" The prioritization of one's own facts and experiences they have gathered, even if they are indeed incorrect, is one of the most common (and most problematic) manifestations of 3R that you'll see in individuals.
