The human mind is not a random stream of consciousness, in which major and everyday decisions are made on a whim based on arbitrary pre-determined factors. Rather, there are 5 psychic Forces in 5 different positions which drive people and influence their personalities. But before we get into these Forces and positions, there are underlying aspects of the psyche and how we as humans make decisions that drive these Forces.

Whenever an individual makes a decision, there are four major factors that they take into account:

- What do I think of this?
- How do I feel about this?
- What even is it that I'm making a decision on?
- Why am I making this decision?

# The Nonhuman Axis and its Poles

**Unorganized thoughts:**

- The Nonhuman Axis represents what the world gives to us: its laws, its rules, and its methods.

## The Objection Pole

**Unorganized thoughts:**

- Objection represents the fact/evidence axis.
- Objection is descriptive: "What is *actually* true/workable?"

## The Subjection Pole

**Unorganized thoughts:**

- Subjection represents the opinion/judgement axis.
- Subjection is normative: "What *should* be true/right?"
- Subjection can be thought of as *how or why* the world works the way it does.

## The Equator

**Unorganized thoughts:**

- The Equator represents balance; neither Objection nor Subjection thrives or dies here.
