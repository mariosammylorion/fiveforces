# Executive Summary

The combination of a private realm of emotional and valuational turmoil along with an intellectual curiosity and a love for outside opinions and knowledge creates the OLMVR, an individual who wishes to seek out knowledge that relates to their struggles. They use their knowledge of their own inner world to relate to what others have to say as well, creating a sense of comfort in knowing that their pain is logically justified. In fact, they use others logical justifications to make excuses as to why they suffer the way they do; therefore, at worst, the views of others become their ultimate scapegoat for getting out of commitment.

# Motivation Development

Because the OLMVR's motivation operates under a loop, any Atom can develop first in the individual. However, the Withdrawn Atom is usually developed first, so an example of that shall be given.

Early in the life of the OLMVR individual, there is a need to find personal meaning and significance in something. Yet for whatever reason, due to reasons relating to heartbreak, grief, or loss, they just do not have a means of finding it. Not only that, but they refuse to seek help for this struggle, instead choosing to simply live through it and figure it out themselves. This makes them retreat into their own Imagination, where there is a strong sense of fear relating to others finding out their struggles. This translates into a fear of others seeing what goes inside their own mind.

Them being exclusively inside their own mind gives them a sense of being on a higher plane of existence. In their eyes, they live in their own reality, detached from everyone else's viewpoint. However, it is strange in the sense that they are not afraid of others' perception of reality; in fact, they greatly admire those who have their own facts and experiences straight, even if they don't care about getting their own straight. This creates a sense of "admiring from afar" when it comes to the facts of reality; they don't engage, but they don't hate, either.

Of course, them admiring reality from afar gives them a penchant curiosity when it comes to those sort of matters, particularly in intellectual fields. Thus, their respect turns from a detached understanding to a desire to seek out logical information without resistance. They love information, and they would do anything to obtain any part of it. Any kind of logic or objectivity, the OLMVR welcomes with open arms. In their eyes, no opinion shall be left unheard or unsaid, and everyone, no matter how ridiculous or wrong, should have a turn to convince them about the current state of intellectual affairs.

However, there is a special nuance and undertone that comes with this curiosity. Keep in mind: the OLMVR individual is still suffering internally. For every objective piece of information that they receive, they can use it to their advantage. When someone asks why they supposedly don't care or that they suffer, they can just dig out any of the opinions that they have gathered and use that as an excuse. This unfortunately only further alienates the OLMVR from finding what is significant in their lives, creating a positive-feedback loop that involves the failure to find what matters and the information that justifies it. 

Eventually, this turns into the distinctive pattern of the psyche, where the individual curates their lives so that only the opinions that they can use to justify their suffering make it through to their peripheral vision. They seek out information that only fuels their excuses, and justifies their suffering. Once they have the information they need from reality, they retreat into their own reality in order to fully encapsulates what it logically means to not find personal meaning, and what the world objectively has to say about those who are less fortunate to find something that they can hold near and dear to their heart.

## Deca Influence

Like all Prima types, the OLMVR is an intellectual type who aims to generate their own novel ideas in order to make sense of the world around them. However, this type is unique when it comes to exactly *what or who* they do this for. Whereas most Prima types do so in order to innovate the world around them, the OLMVR does so to innovate *their* world. They feel as if the only way to truly understand their emotional and valuational suffering is to intellectualize it; as such, their ultimate goal is to logically make sense of their own inner turmoil.

To many Prima-typed individuals, their own mind (and the world around them) is like a puzzle that they must solve through the usage of logical and objective pieces that do not do much on their own. However, when put together, they form a complex system that they can utilize to change any kind of environment, for better or worse. Though the OLMVR is usually self-centered, if healthy enough, they can use the system that they have created for themselves to share it with others, thus making known a revolutionary new way to think about the condition of human suffering.

As such, it is not at all uncommon to see many representatives of the OLMVR type take an interest in existentialist philosophy, as it is the perfect field to utilize all of their skills and motivations in. Existentialism deals primarily with the struggle to lead a life using primarily one's subjective values, despite there being logical explanations as to why this may not truly be possible. This mirrors the motivation of the OLMVR *exactly.* In a way, all Prima types want to find the answers to life, but only the OLMVR gets into the heart of exactly *why* the world prevents them from getting what they truly want.
